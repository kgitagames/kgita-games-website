About the game

Once, you were loved. Now, with corrupted memories and failing power systems, you have been thrown into a pitch-black abandoned city. Hyetyn is a 2D top-down stealth survival game about outrunning the dark: collect all 5 batteries to activate the escape pod, and uncover why you were left behind.

Beat the clock

Your systems are failing. Every battery you find adds 30 seconds to your life.

Use the shadows

Navigate the dark with limited light and evade the patrolling water bubbles.

One touch ends it

A single touch short-circuits you and ends the run instantly.

Power the escape pod

Collect all 5 batteries to activate the pod and break out of the city.
